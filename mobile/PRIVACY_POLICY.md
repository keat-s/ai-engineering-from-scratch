## Who we are

AI Engineering from Scratch ("we", "the app") is an educational app that
delivers a programming/AI curriculum with quizzes and progress tracking.

## What we collect and why

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app, show ads on the
free tier, and process purchases.

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Learning progress, quiz scores | Stored **on your device** | Track which lessons you've completed. Not uploaded to us. |
| Purchase / subscription status | Apple / Google + RevenueCat | Unlock Pro, restore purchases, prevent fraud. |
| Advertising identifier & ad interaction data | Google AdMob | Show ads on the free tier and measure them. With your consent (and, on iOS, App Tracking Transparency permission), ads may be personalized; otherwise they are non-personalized. |
| Device & diagnostic data | Ad/purchase SDKs | Security, fraud prevention, crash diagnostics. |

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do not require an account, and we do
not collect your name or email through the app.

## Third-party services

- **Google AdMob** — ad serving. See Google's Privacy Policy and "How Google
  uses information from sites or apps that use our services."
- **RevenueCat** — subscription/purchase management. See RevenueCat's Privacy
  Policy.
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — payment processing.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

## Your choices

- **Ad personalization / consent:** In the app, open **Settings → Ad & data
  privacy choices** to review or change your consent (GDPR/CCPA via Google's
  consent platform).
- **iOS tracking:** Manage in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- **Reset advertising ID:** In your device's ad settings.
- **Delete on-device data:** Uninstalling the app removes locally stored
  progress.

## Children

The app is intended for a general/professional audience and is **not directed
to children** under the age required by your jurisdiction (e.g. 13 in the US).
We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Data retention

On-device progress remains until you delete the app. Purchase records are
retained by the stores and RevenueCat per their policies.

## International users

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own by the third-party
services listed above.
